About

Built by artists who were once onstage, backstage, and everywhere in between.

Applod began in 2019 as a response to fragmented, inefficient production models. It has since become a full-scale production company designed to streamline complexity and deliver technically sound experiences at any scale.

Positioned as both creative partner and technical lead, we bring precision, agility, and artistry that raises the standard of Filipino-led productions.
